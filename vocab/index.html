Settings

A word comes back for review once it has waited its interval, and the interval grows each time you get it right — so "complete" means three correct recalls spread further and further apart, not three in a row. New words are only introduced when nothing is due and you are under the work-in-progress limit, which is what keeps the buckets draining instead of filling up forever.

…reviews of waiting before a word in that bucket comes back round. A word you get wrong lands in Attempted and is queued to return soon, rather than dissolving back into a quarter of a million words you will never meet again.

Chance of drawing the next word from each bucket. Buckets with nothing in them are skipped and their share is spread over the rest. Complete is never drawn from.

Minimum repeat gap — a word will not come round again until this many other words have been reviewed, whichever mode is running. If a bucket is smaller than the gap, its least-recently-seen word is used instead, so the gap can never deadlock a draw.

Google lookup. Google refuses to be put in an iframe (x-frame-options: SAMEORIGIN), so a plain embed renders blank — as do DuckDuckGo, Bing and Brave. The one sanctioned way to show Google results inside a page is a Programmable Search Engine. Make one (free) at programmablesearchengine.google.com, set it to search the whole web, and paste its ID here — the lookup then opens as a panel on this page. Leave it blank and lookups open in a separate search window instead.

Saved definitions. The same two layers as discards. vocab/definitions.txt is shown to every visitor in place of the dictionary's entry; anything you write here is yours alone until it is published, and overrides the shared file on this machine while it waits.

Discarded words. Discard drops a word from the rotation for good — for entries not worth anybody's time. Two layers: the shared list in vocab/discarded.txt, which is removed for every visitor, and your own discards, which are removed only in this browser until they are published. A static site cannot write to itself, so promoting yours to everyone's means pasting them into that file and deploying.
