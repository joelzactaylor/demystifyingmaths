# Hand-written definitions shown to everyone, in place of the dictionary's.
#
# One per line:   word<TAB>definition
#
# The separator is a single tab. Inside the definition, \n means a line break
# and \\ means a literal backslash — nothing else is escaped. Blank lines and
# lines starting with # are ignored. A word appearing twice keeps the last one.
#
# The page fetches this on load, so anything here is what every visitor sees for
# that word. A visitor's own saved definition still wins over this file, on their
# machine, until they publish it. Use "Copy my definitions" in the page's
# Settings panel to get lines to paste in, then commit and deploy.
