Before this page: list the multiples of a number, write a number as a product of primes using prime factorisation, and compare two index forms to find the highest common factor.

The first number both counts reach

A common multiple of two numbers is a multiple of each of them. Counting in 8s reaches 8, 16, 24, 32, 40 and 48; counting in 12s reaches 12, 24, 36 and 48. Both counts reach 24 and 48, and 24 is the first: the lowest common multiple, written LCM.

LCM of 8 and 12 = 24

The LCM is the smallest number both counts reach

  1. 24 is a multiple of both: 24 = 3 × 8 and 24 = 2 × 12.
  2. No smaller number is a multiple of both: 8 and 16 are not multiples of 12, and 12 is not a multiple of 8.

The lists stay short while the numbers are small. For 120 and 252 the multiples of 120 reach 2,520 at the 21st entry and the multiples of 252 at the 10th: 31 entries written before the first number both lists hold.

Build a multiple of both from prime factors

120 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 and 252 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 7. Every multiple of 252 contains every prime factor of 252: two 2s, two 3s and a 7. 120 already contains the two 2s and one of the 3s, so a multiple of 120 becomes a multiple of 252 once the missing 3 and the missing 7 are multiplied in: 120 × 3 × 7 = 2,520. Nothing else was multiplied in, so 2,520 is the smallest multiple of 120 that is also a multiple of 252.

Adding to 120 what 252 needs

Both numbers as rings of prime factors

120 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 and 252 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 7, with one sector for each prime factor.

Compare the prime factorisations

Written in index form, 120 = 2^3 × 3 × 5 and 252 = 2^2 × 3^2 × 7. Each index counts the factors of one prime, and the two counts of each prime are compared side by side: a common multiple needs the larger count of each.

The LCM of 120 and 252

Write both numbers in index form

120 = 2³ × 3 × 5 and 252 = 2² × 3² × 7, with equal primes in the same column.

  1. Factorise both numbers into index form, with prime bases in ascending order.
  2. Compare the two indices of each prime, counting a missing prime as index 0, and take the higher.
  3. Multiply the chosen prime powers. Every prime in either number is in the product.

Take every prime to its higher index

A multiple of 120 contains at least three 2s, a 3 and a 5. 120 × 7 = 840 = 2^3 × 3 × 5 × 7 still contains three factors of 2, and 2^2 × 3 × 5 = 60 is not a multiple of 120, because it contains only two.

A common multiple of 120 and 252 contains every prime factor of each number. For each prime, it contains at least as many factors of that prime as the number with more. 120 has three factors of 2 and 252 has two, so a common multiple has at least three: the higher index. The smallest common multiple has exactly that many, and the same holds for every prime. The lower index fails the test: 2^2 × 3^2 × 5 × 7 = 1,260 is not a multiple of 120, because 1,260 ÷ 120 = 10.5.

252 contains no factor of 5, so 5^0 is written in 252. The higher of 1 and 0 is 1, so the LCM contains one 5.

120 = 2^3 × 3^1 × 5^1 × 7^0
252 = 2^2 × 3^2 × 5^0 × 7^1
LCM = 2^3 × 3^2 × 5^1 × 7^1

LCM = the product of every prime to its higher index

  1. A prime missing from one number has index 0 there, so the other index is the higher: 5 and 7 both divide 2,520.
  2. A prime with the same index in both keeps that index: 108 and 540 both contain 3^3, so their LCM does too.

No shared prime

125 = 5^3 and 144 = 2^4 × 3^2 have no prime in common: they are coprime. Every prime power of each is in the LCM, so LCM = 125 × 144 = 18,000: the product of the two numbers.

One number is a factor of the other

108 = 2^2 × 3^3 and 540 = 2^2 × 3^3 × 5. Every prime power in 108 is also in 540, so LCM = 540: the larger number itself.

The LCM is never more than the product

120 × 252 = 30,240 is a multiple of 120 and of 252, so the LCM is at most 30,240. In prime factors, 30,240 = 2^5 × 3^3 × 5 × 7: every prime factor of 120 and every prime factor of 252, all together. The two 2s and the 3 that both numbers contain are in the product twice, once from each number, and a common multiple needs only one copy of each: three 2s where the product has five, two 3s where it has three.

What the product holds twice

Both numbers as rings of prime factors

120 has five prime factors and 252 has five, with one sector for each: ten sectors in all.

The surplus is one copy of each prime the two numbers share: 2 × 2 × 3 = 12, and 30,240 ÷ 12 = 2,520. Any shared prime makes the LCM smaller than the product; only coprime numbers, with no prime in common, have nothing surplus and an LCM equal to their product.

Every common multiple is a multiple of the LCM

The common multiples of 120 and 252 are exactly the multiples of 2,520. A common multiple contains at least three 2s, two 3s, a 5 and a 7, so it contains 2^3 × 3^2 × 5 × 7 as a factor: 2,520 divides every one of them.

Divide the LCM by both numbers to check

2,520 ÷ 120 = 21 = 3 × 7
2,520 ÷ 252 = 10 = 2 × 5

Both divisions are exact, so 2,520 is a common multiple; the quotients share no prime, so it is the lowest. A prime common to both quotients shows that the multiple found is too large: divided by that prime, it is still a multiple of both numbers. 30,240 ÷ 120 = 252 and 30,240 ÷ 252 = 120 share the primes 2 and 3, which is how the product fails.

Find the LCM of two chosen numbers

600 and 252 share the primes 2 and 3; 125 and 144 share none; 108 and 540 share every prime of 108. In each case the LCM takes every prime to the higher of its two indices. A prime with index 0 on one side keeps the index it has on the other.

LCM of 600 and 252 = 2³ × 3² × 5² × 7 = 12,600

600 × 252 = 151,200, which is 12 times the LCM: one copy of each shared prime is surplus.

Take the higher index, for every prime

  • Taking the lower index Choosing 2^2 × 3 for 120 and 252 gives 12, which is smaller than both numbers and a multiple of neither. The lower index belongs to the highest common factor, not the LCM.
  • Keeping only the primes both numbers have Taking 2 and 3 to their higher indices and leaving out the 5 and the 7 gives 2^3 × 3^2 = 72. 5 does not divide 72, so 120 does not either.
  • Multiplying the two numbers 120 × 252 = 30,240 is a common multiple, and it is 12 times the lowest one. The product is the LCM only when the two numbers share no prime.
  • Writing the larger number 252 is the LCM of 120 and 252 only if 120 divides it, and 252 = 2 × 120 + 12. The larger number is the LCM when the smaller is a factor of it, as 108 is of 540.

Summary

  1. A common multiple is a multiple of both numbers; the LCM is the smallest common multiple.
  2. For small numbers, list the multiples of each and pick the first that appears in both lists.
  3. For larger numbers, write both in index form; the LCM is every prime in either number, each to its higher index.
  4. The product of the two numbers is a common multiple, so the LCM is at most the product; the two are equal only when no prime is shared.
  5. Divide the LCM by both numbers: both divisions are exact, and the quotients share no prime.
