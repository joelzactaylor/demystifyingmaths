Before this page: be able to list the factors of a number in pairs, and to divide and see what is left over from short division.

Counting up in steps

A multiple of 6 is a number counting in 6s reaches. The list runs 6, 12, 18, 24, and it never ends, because the counting never has to stop.

24 sits in that list, and 6 divides it exactly. Those are one fact read from its two ends: 24 is a multiple of 6, and 6 is a factor of 24.

Multiples run outwards; factors run inwards

  1. The factors of 24 run from 1 up to 24, and there are eight of them.
  2. Counting up in 24s never has to stop, so the list of multiples never ends.
  3. 24 is the largest of its own factors and the first number its own count reaches.

Where two lists meet

Count in 4s and in 6s along one line. Some places are reached by both, and those are the common multiples of 4 and 6.

Find the common multiples of 4 and 6 up to 36

Two counts along one line

Counting in 4s and counting in 6s land together at some places and nowhere else.

12, 24 and 36

12 is the first place the two counts meet. That makes it the lowest common multiple of 4 and 6, and every later meeting is a multiple of it.

How far a test has to look

Testing 4,518 for a factor by dividing takes a whole calculation. A test reads the answer off the digits instead, and how many of them it has to read depends on the number being tested for.

Every ten is 2 fives and 5 twos. So 4,510 is a multiple of 2, of 5 and of 10 before its last digit is written, and only that digit can spoil it.

  1. 2 the last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8 every ten is 5 twos
  2. 5 the last digit is 0 or 5 every ten is 2 fives
  3. 10 the last digit is 0 every ten is 1 ten
  4. 4 the last two digits make a multiple of 4 100 = 4 × 25, so every hundred is 25 fours

10 is not a whole number of 4s. A stray ten can therefore spoil what the hundreds had already settled, so the 4 test reads both of the last two digits. 4,518 ends in 8, which is a multiple of 4, but its last two digits make 18, which is not.

Why adding the digits works

No power of ten is a whole number of 9s. Each one is one more than a number that is, though: 10 is 9 + 1, 100 is 99 + 1, and 1,000 is 999 + 1.

Test 4,518 for a factor of 9

4,518, taken apart by place

Every place splits into a part made of 9s and a single digit left over.

4 + 5 + 1 + 8 = 18, so 4,518 = 9 × 502

999, 99 and 9 are multiples of 3 as well. The same sum therefore answers both questions at once: 18 is a multiple of 3 and of 9, and so is 4,518.

When the sum is still too big to read

Tip

The digits of 18 can be added in their turn. 1 + 8 = 9, and each round leaves a smaller number in the same list, so keep going until the answer is small enough to recognise.

One number, every test

Example: which of 2, 3, 4, 5, 9 and 10 divide 4,518?

Read each answer off the digits rather than dividing.

  1. 8 — by 2, yes The last digit is even, and every ten above it is already a whole number of 2s.
  2. 8 — by 5 and 10, no Those tests want a last digit of 0, or of 5 for the 5 test.
  3. 18 — by 4, no The last two digits are 18, and 4 does not divide 18. The hundreds above them were already whole fours.
  4. 4 + 5 + 1 + 8 = 18 — by 3 and 9, yes 18 is in both lists, so 4,518 is too.
  5. 2, 3 and 9 divide it; 4, 5 and 10 do not Six questions answered by reading four short numbers off the digits, and not one division done.

Common mistakes

  • Swapping factor and multiple 6 is a factor of 24, not a multiple of it. The multiples of 6 are the numbers counting in 6s reaches: 6, 12, 18, 24, and none of them is smaller than 6.
  • Adding the digits for a test that does not use them The digits of 4,527 add to 18, which is a multiple of 9, and 4,527 is odd. Adding the digits tests 3 and 9; the 2 test reads the last digit alone.
  • Testing 4 on the last digit alone 518 ends in 8, and 8 is a multiple of 4, but 518 ÷ 4 = 129.5. Both of the last two digits count: 18 is no good, while 516 works because 16 is.
  • Taking any common multiple for the lowest one 24 is reached by counting in 4s and by counting in 6s, but the two counts have already met at 12. The lowest common multiple is the first meeting, not any of them.

Remember

  1. A multiple of 6 is a number counting up in 6s reaches, and 6 is a factor of every one of them.
  2. A common multiple of 4 and 6 is in both lists, and the lowest is 12.
  3. Every ten is a whole number of 2s, 5s and 10s, so those tests read the last digit alone.
  4. Every hundred is 25 fours, so the 4 test reads the last two digits: 18 for 4,518.
  5. 9, 99 and 999 are all multiples of 3 and of 9, so those tests add the digits: 4 + 5 + 1 + 8 = 18.
