Drills

Subtracting in columns with exchange, lining up decimals of different lengths.

Author note — to cover

Column subtraction of integers and decimals with exchange, aligning points and padding shorter decimals, e.g. 12 - 3.475; change and difference contexts. Exchanges that must pass through zero digits get the sibling page; answers below zero live on adding-subtracting-negatives. Both tiers. (N2)

  • Fluency — subtract integers and decimals of different lengths by aligning place values and padding zeros (4 questions).
  • Exchange — subtract with ordinary single-column exchanges, excluding any exchange that must pass through a zero (4 questions).
  • Application — solve change and difference problems with non-negative answers and no across-zero exchange (4 questions).
