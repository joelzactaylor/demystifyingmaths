Before this page: know how to multiply and divide by powers of ten, and know basic multiplication and division facts.

One given calculation gives many more

In 43 × 26 = 1,118, scaling one factor scales the product with it; “given that 43 × 26 = 1,118, work out 4.3 × 26” asks for that scaling.

For 4.3 × 26, compare each value with the given calculation:

  1. The first factor 43 → 4.3 Divided by 10.
  2. The second factor 26 → 26 Unchanged.
  3. The product 1,118 → 111.8 Divided by 10 as well.

Scale one factor at a time, then combine the changes

Write each change beside the number it affects, ÷ 10 beside 43 → 4.3 and unchanged beside 26, so the product's change is read off before its decimal point is placed.

Given: 43 × 26 = 1,118

Begin with the given calculation

Every related fact is 43 × 26 = 1,118 with one or both factors scaled by a power of ten.

A tenth of a tenth is a hundredth, so ÷ 10 on both factors is ÷ 100 on the product: 1,118 → 11.18.

product change = first-factor change × second-factor change

An unchanged factor contributes ×1.

Read a product fact backwards

Every multiplication fact gives two division facts. From 43 × 26 = 1,118 come:

product 43 × 26 = 1,118
divide by 43 1,118 ÷ 43 = 26
divide by 26 1,118 ÷ 26 = 43

To find a quotient, form the related multiplication fact first, then read it backwards:

Same product, smaller divisor

4.3 × 260 = 1,118

4.3 is one tenth of 43. To keep the product at 1,118, the other factor must be ten times 26.

1,118 ÷ 4.3 = 260

Product and divisor both smaller

43 × 2.6 = 111.8

2.6 is one tenth of 26 and 111.8 is one tenth of 1,118, so the other factor stays 43.

111.8 ÷ 2.6 = 43

Rewrite a missing value with an inverse

A missing factor, dividend or divisor is found the same way. First use an inverse operation to make the missing value the subject. Then write each changed number as the given one scaled: 4.3 as 43 ÷ 10, 260 as 26 × 10.

Common mistakes

  • Changing the product only once Dividing both factors by 10 divides the product by 10 twice: from 4.3 × 2.6 = 11.18, 0.43 × 0.26 = 0.1118, not 1.118.
  • Moving every decimal point the same way 43 → 4.3 is ÷ 10, and moving the quotient the same way gives 1,118 ÷ 4.3 = 2.6. Dividing by a tenth of 43 gives ten times the quotient: 1,118 ÷ 4.3 = 260, not 2.6.

Summary

  1. Treat the stated calculation as a fact that connects two factors and their product.
  2. Write how each factor has changed: ×10, ÷10, ×100, ÷100 or unchanged.
  3. Combine the two factor changes to find the product's change: ÷10 on both factors is ÷100 on the product.
  4. Every multiplication fact gives two division facts: 1,118 ÷ 4.3 = 260 because 4.3 × 260 = 1,118.
  5. Use inverse operations to make a missing factor, dividend or divisor the subject, then scale each number from the given one.
