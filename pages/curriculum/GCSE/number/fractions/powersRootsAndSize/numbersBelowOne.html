Author note — to cover

The size effects of multiplying or dividing by a number between 0 and 1, fraction or decimal: multiplying shrinks, dividing enlarges; which-is-bigger judgements and estimation checks, no new algorithms. Underpins dividing-by-decimals and over-under-estimates; cross-reference both rather than reteach. Both tiers. (N2, N14)
