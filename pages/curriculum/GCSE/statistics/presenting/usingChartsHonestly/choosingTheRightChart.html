Author note — to cover

Match diagram to data type: bar chart or pie for categorical, vertical line chart for ungrouped discrete, polygon or frequency diagram for grouped continuous, line graph for time series, scatter for bivariate, and justify or reject a choice. Single-choice and explain-why questions. Higher students should see histograms among the options. (S2, S4)
