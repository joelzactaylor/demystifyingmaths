Author note — to cover

Criticise misleading presentations: truncated or missing frequency axes, uneven scales, 3-D and area distortion in picture charts, unlabelled axes. Question type: state precisely why the impression exaggerates and give the honest reading. Interpretation only, no drawing. This serves the know-their-appropriate-use clause; keep criticisms specific, never vague. Both tiers. (S2)
