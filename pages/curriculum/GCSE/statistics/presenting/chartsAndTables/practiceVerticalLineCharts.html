The chart for ungrouped discrete numerical data, with bars shrunk to lines.

Author note — to cover

Draw and interpret vertical line charts for ungrouped discrete numerical data, with the numerical axis making the mode and range visible. Contrast with bar charts: lines not bars because the horizontal axis is numerical. Grouped or continuous data is out of scope here and needs polygons or histograms instead. Both tiers. (S2)
