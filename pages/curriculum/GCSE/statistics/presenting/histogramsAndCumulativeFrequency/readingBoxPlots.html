Author note — to cover (Higher)

Higher only. Read a drawn box plot: the five values, range and interquartile range, plus statements like a quarter of the data lies above the upper quartile. Confront the trap of the box width read as the range. Comparing two box plots in context belongs to Interpretation; skewness language is optional at GCSE. (S4)
