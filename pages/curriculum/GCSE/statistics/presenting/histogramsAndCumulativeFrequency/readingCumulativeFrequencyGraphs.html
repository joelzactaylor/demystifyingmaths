Higher only. Read a cumulative frequency curve: estimate the median at n over 2, quartiles at n over 4 and 3n over 4, the interquartile range, and how many values lie above or below a given value. Stress these are estimates. Quartile definitions live on Averages and spread; box plots drawn from the curve are the sibling page. (S3, S4)
