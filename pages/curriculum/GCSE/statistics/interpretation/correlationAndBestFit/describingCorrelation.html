Author note — to cover

Describe correlation from a scatter graph: positive, negative or none, strong or weak, and translate the description into context, such as taller people tending to be heavier. Interpretation only, plotting sits on Presenting data. No correlation coefficients or ranked correlation; numerical measures are A level and GCSE Statistics. Both tiers. (S6)
