Author note — to cover

Criticise and improve survey questions: leading wording, missing time frame, overlapping or non-exhaustive response boxes, no option for zero or other. Include write-an-improved-question tasks. The 2013 DfE content no longer names questionnaire design, so treat it as a supporting skill, not exam-critical. Sampling bias lives on Bias in sampling. Both tiers. (S1)
