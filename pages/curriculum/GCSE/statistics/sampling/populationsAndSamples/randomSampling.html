Author note — to cover

Teach simple random sampling: number the population list, use random numbers or a draw, equal chance for every member. Questions describe a valid method step by step and explain why haphazard selection is not random. Exclude stratified sampling entirely, removed from 9-1 GCSE and now A level. Both tiers. (S1)
