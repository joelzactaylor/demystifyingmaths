Author note — to cover

Multiply column and letter vectors by scalars, fractions and negatives included: the length scales while the direction holds or reverses, and expressions like 2a minus 3b are evaluated componentwise. The geometric consequence — scalar multiple means parallel — is developed on the Higher Parallel vectors page. Both tiers. (G25)
