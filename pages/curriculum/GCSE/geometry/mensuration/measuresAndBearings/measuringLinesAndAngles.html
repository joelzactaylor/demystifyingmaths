Author note — to cover

Measure and draw segments to the nearest millimetre and angles to the nearest degree, choosing the protractor scale that starts from zero, with reflex angles by subtraction from 360. Estimate first to catch gross errors. Compass constructions live on Properties; bearings follow later in this topic. Both tiers. (G15)
