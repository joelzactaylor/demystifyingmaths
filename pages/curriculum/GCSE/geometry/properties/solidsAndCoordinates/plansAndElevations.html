Author note — to cover

Draw the plan, front and side elevations of cuboid-built and simple curved solids on squared paper, and reconstruct or sketch the solid from its three views — G13 requires both directions. State which direction each elevation faces. Isometric drawing may support but is exam-board convention, not DfE. Both tiers. (G13)
