Author note — to cover

The locus a fixed distance from a point is a circle, and from a segment a stadium shape of two parallels and two semicircular ends; draw both accurately with compasses, including inside scale-drawing contexts. Regions inside or outside a boundary wait for Combining loci. Both tiers. (G2)
