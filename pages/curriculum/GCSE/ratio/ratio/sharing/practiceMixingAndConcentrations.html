Drills

Ratios in paint mixes and concentrations, and comparing mixture strengths.

Author note — to cover

Cover paint, mortar, squash and alloy mixtures: scaling a mix, finding one ingredient from another, comparing strengths via unit fractions or 1 : n, and limited-ingredient maxima. Deciding same-strength is the key question type. Density of a mixture sits under Compound measures; serving-based recipes are Proportion's scaling-a-recipe. Both tiers. (R5)
