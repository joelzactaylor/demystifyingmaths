  7. Part:part and part:whole ratios

Author note — to cover

Cover distinguishing part:part from part:whole statements, converting each to the other in two- and three-part cases, and decoding wordings like 'one in five' or '2 boys for every 3 girls'. Sharing a quantity given either form is dividing-in-a-ratio; writing parts as fractions is fractions-of-the-whole. Both tiers. (R5)
