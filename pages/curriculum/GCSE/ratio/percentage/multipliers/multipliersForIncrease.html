Author note — to cover

Cover forming an increase multiplier by adding to 100% and writing as a decimal: 12% gives x1.12, 4.5% gives x1.045, a 150% rise gives x2.5, applied in one calculator step. Percentages above 100% included. Decreases and decoding multipliers are the sibling pages; repeated application waits for repeated-change. Both tiers. (R9)
