Drills

Subtracting the percentage from 100%, so a 15% fall becomes ×0.85, not ×0.15.

Author note — to cover

Cover forming a decrease multiplier by subtracting from 100%, so 15% off gives x0.85, applying it in one step, and killing the x0.15 error explicitly. Include small decimal rates like 2.5%. Increases are the sibling page; dividing by the multiplier to reverse a change belongs to the reverse-problems group. Both tiers. (R9)
