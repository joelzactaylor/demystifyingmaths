Author note — to cover

Read the fixed charge as intercept and the rate as gradient on tariff, wage and unit-conversion graphs; compare two tariffs and interpret the crossing point as break-even. Gradient and area interpretation on curved financial graphs falls under the Higher A15 braces on gradients-and-areas. Both tiers. (A14)
