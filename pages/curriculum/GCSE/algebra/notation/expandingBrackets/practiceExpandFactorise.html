Drills

Multiplying every term in one bracket by every term in the other.

Author note — to cover

Expand (x + a)(x + b) in all four sign combinations by grid or FOIL, collecting the two middle terms. Coefficients of x stay 1 here: (2x + 3)(3x − 1) is the sibling page, squaring a bracket its own page, and three brackets the Higher page. Both tiers. (A4)
