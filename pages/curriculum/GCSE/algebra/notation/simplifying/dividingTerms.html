Author note — to cover

Write divisions as fractions and cancel common numeric and letter factors, e.g. 12ab ÷ 3a = 4b, including quotients that leave a fractional coefficient. Boundaries: subtracting indices, like 12a⁵ ÷ 4a², is the dividing-powers page; fractions with polynomial numerators are the Higher algebraic-fractions pages. Both tiers. (A4)
