Author note — to cover

Recognise and continue 1, 3, 6, 10 from the add-one-more rule and the dot-triangle picture; find several further terms and pick triangular numbers out of given values. The formula n(n + 1)/2 may be quoted, but deriving quadratic nth terms is the Higher group. Both tiers. (A24)
