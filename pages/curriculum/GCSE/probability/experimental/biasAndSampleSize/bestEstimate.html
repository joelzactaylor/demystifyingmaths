Author note — to cover

Choose the best estimate as the one backed by the most trials, and pool several result sets by totalling successes over totalled trials, never averaging the separate fractions. Justify-the-choice question type. Both tiers. Exclude formal sampling theory and confidence intervals, which are A level statistics. (P5)
