Author note — to cover

Define independence as one outcome giving no information about the other; classify pairs such as coin then dice against successive picks without replacement; separate independence from mutual exclusivity. Both tiers. Exclude the multiplication itself (and-rule page) and testing independence by comparing P(A and B) with the product, an A level idea. (P8)
