Author note — to cover

One probability written as a fraction, a decimal and a percentage, converting all three ways, and recognising equal values such as 3/20, 0.15 and 15 percent. Warn that ratio answers like 1:6 score nothing. Both tiers. Exclude calculating probabilities (equally-likely-outcomes) and deciding which is larger (comparing-probabilities). (P3)
