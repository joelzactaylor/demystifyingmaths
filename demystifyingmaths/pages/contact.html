Get in touch

Please don't spam me or anything, but I'd love to hear from anyone about anything on this site, or any aspects of maths.

I'm not aged enough yet to consider interesting things a waste of my time, and luckily I find everything about maths interesting, so don't hesitate to ask if you have even the most insignificant question or suggestion.

If there's anything you think I can help with, please reach out. I've answered a lot of questions before and people's questions generally are more intelligent than they think. The percentage of them that are prefaced with "This is probably a stupid question, but..." is astounding. However, I have not come across a question yet that deserved that statement, so yours probably doesn't either. Send it over!


No need to overthink it. Good questions do not have to sound impressive to be worth asking.
