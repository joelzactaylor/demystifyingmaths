# Whole questions from published papers

The intended direction is complete, properly attributed questions, not snippets
rewritten into a different task. Include every part only when **all** parts are
covered by the subtopic and its already-taught prerequisites.

The current two revision pages still contain original questions. They do not
contain licensed GCSE past-paper screenshots. Do not describe them as past papers.

  These are **KS2**, not GCSE. Do not silently substitute them for GCSE papers.

## Import requirements

Record publisher, qualification, year, paper, question number, PDF page, source
URL, reuse terms, required attribution and the coverage of every part. Check
diagrams visually and answers independently against the official mark scheme.
Render a sharp crop retaining all question text, diagrams, parts and original
marks; do not redraw a lookalike and call it a screenshot. Provide an equivalent
accessible text version. Place the editable control over the paper's own blank
answer line using measured renderer coordinates; never add a second answer box
outside the crop or obscure printed mathematical content.
Only claim to check the parts actually assessed—never automatically award method
marks from a final answer.

If rights are unclear, link to the publisher instead. Do not send permission
requests, accept licences or pay fees without the owner's approval.
